Control F: the article link opens a popup and nothing else

The link below is intercepted and opened with window.open(). The original tab does not navigate.

Expected: article_test_status = ARTICLE_OPENED_POPUP_ONLY, article_click_opened_new_window = true, and the popup closed with focus returned to this tab.

Why it matters: the old classifier scored the popup itself as hijacking — click_opened_unexpected_new_window_or_tab, 180 of the 352 press-1 records — while Back in the original tab worked perfectly. Measuring Back inside the popup answers a different question, and running a back chain in the original tab after a popup would measure the main page and label the result an article test.
