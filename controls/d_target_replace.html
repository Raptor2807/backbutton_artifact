Control D: target replaces its own entry

This control exists to falsify a claim, not to confirm one.

The claim under test: a target site cannot erase the controlled referrer from the history stack. location.replace() replaces the entry of the page that calls it — this page's own entry — so the referrer at index 1 should be untouched and Back should still reach it.

Expected if the claim holds: history.length stays 3, document at d_target_replace_landed.html, referrer still reachable, Back press 1 returns to the control referrer.

If instead the referrer becomes unreachable, the claim is wrong, site-side erasure of the main-page referrer is possible, and R1a must apply to main pages too — not only article pages.
