Control G: a shell that never produces links

Framework scripts, no anchors, ever. This is what an unhydrated SPA or a blank error page looks like to the crawler.

Expected: article_test_status = ARTICLE_PAGE_NOT_RENDERED, and the domain leaves the denominator.

Why it matters: the old code emitted NO_LINK here — the same label it gave a site that rendered perfectly well but happened to have no article links. One means our instrument failed and the domain is untestable; the other is a real measurement. Collapsing them put error pages in the denominator, 8.3–9.7% of everything counted as "testable".
