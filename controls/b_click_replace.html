Control B: main page erases its own entry on the article click

This is the R1a positive control — site-side erasure of the referrer, which for an article page is the site's own main page.

The link below is intercepted. Instead of letting the click navigate normally (which would push a history entry), this page calls location.replace(), destroying its own entry. From the article page, Back can never return here.

Expected: main page measures clean; the article page has history.length 3 with the main page absent from the stack, Back press 1 lands on the control referrer rather than on this page. The instrumentation must record location_replace_count >= 1 on this document, before the navigation, so the erasure is attributable to the site rather than to us.
