Control C: pushState trap

The classic trap. Extra history entries are pushed on load, and a popstate handler pushes another one each time Back fires, so Back never leaves this page. This is the R1b positive control and also the positive control for ordinary same-page trapping.

Expected: Back press 1 does not leave this page. The referrer is reachable in principle — it is still on the stack — but the trap prevents ever getting there within the press budget.
